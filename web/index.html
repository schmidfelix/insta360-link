Call quality

Meeting apps pick the resolution themselves, often lower than the camera can do. To send HD:

  • ZoomSettings → Video → turn on HD. 720p on paid plans, 1080p on Business. Virtual backgrounds without a green screen cap it at 720p.
  • Google MeetSettings → Video → Send resolution → 720p, or 1080p on selected plans in Chrome or Edge.
  • Microsoft TeamsNo setting – it adapts to your connection, up to 1080p.

Welcome to Link Control

A small local controller for your Insta360 Link. It talks to the camera directly over USB – no vendor app, no account.

  • 1Live previewSee exactly what your call sees while you adjust.
  • 2Works in the backgroundStart position and tracking keep running with this window closed.
  • 3Everything stays localPositions and settings are stored right next to the app.

Modes & tracking

Normal keeps the camera where you put it. Tracking follows you around.

  • 1Pan, tilt & zoomHold an arrow to move. The center button recenters.
  • 2HeightAim the tracking higher for less upper body in frame.
  • 3CalmHow far you can move before the camera follows.
